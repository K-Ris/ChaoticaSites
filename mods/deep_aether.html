Deep Aether

deep_aether · 1.21.1-1.1.5.1 · RazorDevs · deep_aether-1.21.1-1.1.5.1.jar
Dimensions

Deep Aether is an unofficial addon for the Aether Mod which aims to add extra content to the Aether Dimension in a similar fashion to the 1.16 Nether Expansion update. Extra content includes new blocks, items, plants, biomes, trees, mobs, and more! Created by RazorDevs!

Requires: AeroBlender, The Aether
